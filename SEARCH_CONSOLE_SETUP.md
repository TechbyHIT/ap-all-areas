# Google Search Console Setup

1. Verify ownership of the production domain.
2. Submit `https://your-domain/sitemap.xml`.
3. Monitor Coverage / Pages reports for indexed vs excluded URLs.
4. Watch for duplicate title/description and soft-404 warnings.
5. Expand publishing only after Phase 1 URLs show healthy indexing and no mass duplicate issues.

Do not force exactly 50,000 live URLs when quality is insufficient.
