# Adding Areas

1. Attach areas to a parent city via `Area` (`locationId` + unique `slug`).
2. High-priority city sub-areas are seeded from `HIGH_PRIORITY_CITY_AREAS`.
3. Leave bulk areas non-indexable until verified.
4. Create area pages and area-service pages only when they add distinct value beyond the parent city page.
5. Include nearby localities and parent city/service links.
